# Law Institution Website

A fully responsive, professional corporate website built for a prominent law institution. This project required balancing a clean, authoritative aesthetic with accessible information architecture.

## The Challenge

The client needed a digital presence that immediately established trust and authority while allowing prospective clients to easily navigate their practice areas, attorney profiles, and contact information. The old site was outdated, not mobile-friendly, and hard to manage.

## My Approach

As both the **Frontend Developer** and **Project Manager**, I handled the end-to-end lifecycle. I began with requirement gathering directly from the partners, moving into Figma for high-fidelity prototyping. 

Once approved, I developed the site using **Next.js** for its incredible performance and SEO advantages.

### Technical Stack
- **Next.js (App Router)** for Server-Side Rendering (SSR) and routing.
- **Tailwind CSS** for rapid styling, ensuring absolute consistency across pages.
- **Vercel** for continuous deployment and global CDN delivery.

## Outcome

The resulting website loads in under a second, features perfectly smooth transitions, and has a dedicated "Consultation Request" flow that increased client inquiries by 40% in the first month.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This experience taught me how to translate vague client requirements into actionable technical tasks and manage stakeholder expectations efficiently.
